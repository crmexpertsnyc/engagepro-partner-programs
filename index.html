EngagePro Partner & Affiliate Programs

Choose the program that fits your business model

Partner Program

If you're a sales professional, consultant, or agency who wants to actively sell and support clients, the Partner Program is the best fit.

As a Partner, you'll receive:

  • Qualified leads directly from EngagePro
  • Bi-weekly training sessions on how and where to sell
  • Technical support from our implementation team
  • 30% recurring commissions on all sales
  • Co-branded materials and a dedicated Partner Portal
  • Access to sandbox and feature training
Partner tiers start at $25/month
Silver, Gold, and Platinum options based on your desired support level and lead volume

Affiliate Program

If you prefer to promote EngagePro CRM online without managing client relationships directly, the Affiliate Program is ideal.

Affiliates earn commissions simply by referring new customers through your unique link.

This program is perfect for:

  • Content creators, influencers, and bloggers
  • Email marketers or newsletter owners
  • Educators or online business advisors

You'll receive:

  • Marketing materials and tracking links
  • A real-time commission dashboard
  • Fast commission payouts per sale
Free to join
Designed for those who focus on content-driven promotion rather than direct selling
